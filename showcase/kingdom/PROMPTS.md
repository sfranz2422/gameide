# Highmoor: Gemini prompts

Highmoor is drawn **realistic**, at the teacher's ask ("less cartoony, more realistic"
than Cloverbrook). Every picture is isometric at the classic 2:1 angle, as Cloverbrook's
are, so the game can stand each one on its tiles.

Paste this style line at the start of every prompt (shown as *[the style]* below):

> Realistic, detailed isometric strategy-game art of a medieval kingdom: true 2:1
> isometric view (each ground diamond exactly twice as wide as it is tall), natural
> muted colours, real stone, timber, thatch and iron textures, soft afternoon sunlight
> from the upper left with gentle shadows, no cartoon outlines, no text, no people.

Ask for a **flat magenta (#FF00FF) background** every time, with clear space between
the drawings: the cutting script keys the magenta out. Download each picture as it
comes; the order below is the order the script expects (raw/01.jpeg, 02 …).

Each building comes in **three ages** (levels 1-3, 4-6 and 7-10), left to right: the
same building, growing grander, on the same size of ground.

**1. The keep.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] ONE row of three, each standing on its own square patch of packed earth
> and grass the size of 4 × 4 ground diamonds: a medieval keep in three ages. First a
> small square stone tower with a timber top and a wooden door; then a taller stone
> keep with battlements, arrow slits and a banner; then a grand castle keep with corner
> turrets, slate roofs and gold-trimmed banners. Flat magenta background.

**2. Gold mines and farms.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Two rows of three, each on its own square patch of ground the size of
> 3 × 3 ground diamonds. Row 1, a gold mine in three ages: a timber-framed mine entrance
> in a rocky mound with a cart of gold ore; the same with a wooden headframe and a pile
> of gold; a large stone-built mine with a wheel hoist and carts of gold. Row 2, a farm
> in three ages: a small wheat field with a wooden shed; a bigger field and a timber
> barn; golden fields, a stone barn and a windmill. Flat magenta background.

**3. Treasuries and granaries.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Two rows of three, each on its own square patch of ground the size of
> 3 × 3 ground diamonds. Row 1, a treasury in three ages: a small stone strongroom with
> an iron-bound door and a chest of gold coins outside; a larger stone vault with iron
> bars and sacks of coins; a grand domed stone treasury with guarded gates and heaps of
> gold. Row 2, a granary in three ages: a small timber granary on stone stilts with
> grain sacks; a bigger granary with a thatched roof and baskets of bread and apples; a
> great stone granary with a tiled roof, carts of grain and barrels. Flat magenta background.

**4. Archer towers and ballistas.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Two rows of three, each on its own square patch of ground the size of
> 3 × 3 ground diamonds. Row 1, an archer tower in three ages: a tall timber watchtower
> with a platform; a stone tower with a wooden hoarding; a tall round stone tower with
> a conical slate roof and arrow slits. Row 2, a ballista (a giant mounted crossbow) in
> three ages: a wooden ballista on a timber platform; a bigger one on a stone base; a
> heavy iron-reinforced ballista on a fortified stone platform. Flat magenta background.

**5. Trebuchets and barracks.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Two rows of three, each on its own square patch of ground the size of
> 3 × 3 ground diamonds. Row 1, a trebuchet in three ages: a small timber trebuchet
> with a stone counterweight; a larger one with iron fittings; a huge war trebuchet on
> a stone base with a pile of round stones. Row 2, barracks in three ages: a timber
> longhouse with a weapon rack and a training dummy; a stone-and-timber barracks with
> shields on the walls; a large stone barracks with a courtyard and banners. Flat
> magenta background.

**6. Army camps, the builder's hut and walls.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Two rows. Row 1: an army camp in three ages, each on its own square patch
> of ground the size of 4 × 4 ground diamonds: a few canvas tents round a campfire; more
> tents with a wooden palisade and a flag; a large camp with striped pavilions, a stone
> fire pit and weapon racks. Row 2: a builder's hut (a small timber cottage with a
> workbench, tools and planks) on a patch the size of 2 × 2 ground diamonds; then a
> single short section of wall in three ages, each on ONE ground diamond, running from
> the top left to the bottom right: a wooden palisade of sharpened logs; a rough stone
> wall; a dressed stone wall with battlements. Flat magenta background.

**7. Trees, rocks and building sites.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Two rows. Row 1, each on ONE small ground diamond of grass: an oak tree; a
> tall pine tree; a leafy bush; a large grey boulder; an old tree stump. Row 2, a
> building site in three sizes, each on bare earth: wooden scaffolding with a few stones
> and planks, on 2 × 2, 3 × 3 and 4 × 4 ground diamonds. Flat magenta background.

**8. Icons.** MAGENTA.
> Realistic, detailed game icons, each a single object on its own small rounded square
> of dark weathered stone with a thin brass edge, all the same size, two rows of five,
> no text. Row 1: a heap of gold coins; a sheaf of wheat and a loaf of bread; a laurel
> wreath (renown); a builder's mallet and chisel; a claw hammer. Row 2: an hourglass; a
> heater shield with a red cross; a steel sword; a green arrow pointing up; a small
> stone castle keep. Flat magenta background.

**9. The title picture.** (No magenta.)
> [the style, but not isometric] A wide 16:9 painting of a medieval kingdom on a high
> moor at golden hour: a walled castle town on a hill, fields and a mill, a misty
> valley and distant mountains, banners in the wind. Leave the top third calm sky for
> the game's name.

## Phase 2: the army

Same chat, same style line. Each troop is drawn in **eight small frames in one row**, the
same figure every time, full body, no ground under them: four walking towards you and to
the LEFT (two steps, then two swings of its weapon), then four walking AWAY from you and
to the LEFT (two steps, two swings). The game turns them round to face right.

**10. Troops, part one.** MAGENTA.
> [the style, but not isometric] A sheet of small full-body medieval soldiers seen from a
> little above, four rows of eight frames, every frame the same size with space between:
> each row one soldier, frames 1-2 walking towards the viewer and to the left, frames 3-4
> swinging their weapon towards the viewer and the left, frames 5-6 walking away from the
> viewer and to the left, frames 7-8 swinging away and to the left. Row 1: a footman in a
> red tunic with a sword, round shield and iron helmet. Row 2: an archer in green with a
> longbow. Row 3: a big man-at-arms in full grey plate armour with a mace. Row 4: a raider
> in a brown hooded leather jerkin with a dagger and a sack. Flat magenta background.

**11. Troops, part two.** MAGENTA.
> [the same as 10] Three rows of eight frames. Row 1: a sapper in a leather cap carrying
> a small black-powder keg with a lit fuse (frames 3-4 and 7-8: lifting the keg to throw
> it). Row 2: a knight in shining armour on a brown warhorse with a lance. Row 3: a
> battering ram: a heavy log with an iron head under a small wooden roof on four wheels,
> pushed by two soldiers (frames 3-4 and 7-8: the log swinging forward). Flat magenta
> background.

**12. Rubble and the battle's icons.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Row 1: rubble of a knocked-down building, broken stone, charred timber and
> dust, on bare earth, in four sizes: 1 × 1, 2 × 2, 3 × 3 and 4 × 4 ground diamonds. Row 2,
> in the style of the icons (picture 8: a small rounded square of dark stone with a brass
> edge): a gold star; a crossed sword and axe; a soldier's iron helmet. Flat magenta background.
