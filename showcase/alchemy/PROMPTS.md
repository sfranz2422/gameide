# Mix Lab: picture prompts for Gemini

A combining game: drag one thing onto another and see what they make. You start with
Water, Fire, Earth and Air and can make 216 things, from Steam and Mud to Dragons,
Rockets and Video Games. What you make also shows up living in a little world at the
top of the screen. The game draws the little world's land, sea and sky itself, so
the pictures are a workbench and the icons.

Every picture will have a stand-in drawn by the build, so the game plays without
any of these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then tell me which are new. I copy them into `showcase/alchemy/raw/` straight away,
so they can't be lost.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything.** Make picture 2 (the first icon sheet) first,
   then attach it to every later icon sheet and add: *"In exactly the same art style,
   outlines, palette and lighting as the attached picture."*
2. **No words, numbers or logos** anywhere.
3. **Icon sheets are on flat magenta (#FF00FF)**, nothing behind each icon, no shadow,
   no circle or tile behind it. The build cuts the magenta away.
4. **Six icons a row, three rows, every icon about the same size, with clear space
   between them**, in exactly the order the prompt lists them. Don't add or swap any.
5. **Each icon is one object, seen from the front**, simple enough to read when small.

## The style (start every icon prompt with this)

> A sheet of 18 bright, glossy cartoon game icons for a cheerful kids' discovery game:
> each one a single object, chunky and simple, clean thick dark outlines, soft shading
> with a shine, rich colours. Six icons in each row, three rows, all the same size,
> evenly spaced with clear space between them, on a flat magenta (#FF00FF) background.
> No text, no letters, no numbers, no shadows, no tiles or circles behind the icons.

## 1. The workbench (no magenta)

> A wide (16:9) cosy wooden workbench seen straight from above, warm honey-coloured
> planks filling the whole picture, a few faint scratches, soft warm light, a small
> brass corner bracket in each corner. Nothing on it at all. Bright cartoon style,
> clean outlines, no text.
