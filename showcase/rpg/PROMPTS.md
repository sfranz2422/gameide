# The Lanterns of Hollowmere: picture prompts for Gemini

The eleventh showcase game: a story RPG with turn-based battles, long enough for
two or three sittings. Every lantern in the valley has gone out, and the dark is
bringing monsters down from the hills.

Every picture has a stand-in drawn by `build.py`, so the game plays without any of
these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like: anything missing stays a
stand-in. Pictures with the same number can come back together on one sheet.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then say which ones are new. Gemini never uses our names, so I look at each one and
name it myself. Unlike the older games, these get copied into `showcase/rpg/art/`
and committed, so they can't be lost the way the older games' art was.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything.** Make picture 1 first, then attach it to every
   later prompt and add: *"In exactly the same art style, outlines, palette and
   lighting as the attached picture."*
2. **No words, numbers or logos** anywhere. The game writes its own.
3. **Anything marked MAGENTA** goes on flat magenta (#FF00FF) with nothing else
   behind it and no shadow on the ground. The build cuts the magenta away.
4. **Leave clear space between drawings on a sheet**, so that they can be cut apart,
   and keep them in the rows the prompt asks for.
5. **Same size:** on a character sheet, every drawing should be the same height with
   its feet on the same line.

## The style (start every prompt with this)

> Clean, chunky 16-bit fantasy role-playing game pixel art, like a classic Super
> Nintendo RPG: visible square pixels, bold dark outlines, warm saturated colours,
> cosy storybook mood. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border.

## The heroes

**1. Wren, walking.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of a small cute full-body pixel art hero, a young sword fighter with short
> auburn hair, a red tunic, brown boots and a short sword on the hip. Two rows of three
> drawings. Top row: facing the viewer, walking back view, walking side view facing
> right. Bottom row: the same three views with the other foot forward. Top-down RPG
> proportions, big head, about two heads tall. Flat magenta background.

**2. Pip, walking.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet layout for a second hero: a small cheerful healer with round glasses,
> a blue hooded robe and a wooden staff with a tiny glowing lantern on top.

**3. Bram, walking.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet layout for a third hero: a big friendly blacksmith with a short black
> beard, a green shirt, a leather apron and a heavy hammer on the back.

**4. The heroes in battle.** MAGENTA.
> Three rows of four drawings, larger and more detailed than a walking sprite, side view
> facing LEFT. Row 1 Wren, row 2 Pip, row 3 Bram (same heroes as attached). In each row:
> standing ready, attacking with their weapon mid-swing, hurt and leaning back, knocked
> down on the ground. Flat magenta background.

## Faces for the dialogue box

**6. Villain portraits.** MAGENTA.

## Wide pictures

Ask for each of these **wide, 16:9**, with **nobody and no monsters in them**, and with the lower
third fairly plain, because the battle stands on it.

**16. Battle in the fields.**
> A side-on view across green fields at dusk, a village on the far hills, as the background of a
> turn-based battle in a Super Nintendo RPG. A flat grassy stage across the lower third.

**17. Battle in the forest.**
> A side-on view into a deep, dim forest with shafts of light, the background of a turn-based battle. A
> flat mossy clearing across the lower third.

**18. Battle in the mine.**
> A side-on view of a mine tunnel with wooden beams and glowing orange crystals, the background of a
> turn-based battle. A flat rocky floor across the lower third.

**19. Battle in the marsh.**
> A side-on view of a misty green marsh with dead trees and pale lights, the background of a turn-based
> battle. A flat muddy bank across the lower third.

**20. Battle in the tower.**
> A side-on view of a ruined throne hall with purple shadow and tall broken windows, the background of a
> turn-based battle. A flat stone floor across the lower third.

**21. Title.**
> A dark valley at night under stars, a small village far below with every window dark, and on a hill
> in front of it one tiny lit lantern. Leave the top third calm and empty, because the title goes there.

**22. The end.**
> The same valley at dawn, with hundreds of warm lanterns lit all through the village and up the hills,
> and three tiny figures on the hill in front looking down at it.
