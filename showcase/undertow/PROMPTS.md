# Undertow: picture prompts for Gemini

The next multiplayer showcase: a hidden-impostor game on a research station at
the bottom of the sea. The crew keep the station running with quick jobs; a few
of them are secretly **Mimics**, deep-sea creatures wearing crew suits, who
freeze people with a cold touch. Meetings, votes, and a lot of darkness.

Every picture will have a stand-in drawn by the build, so the game plays without
any of these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then tell me which are new. I copy them into `showcase/undertow/raw/` straight away,
so they can't be lost.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything.** Make picture 1 first, then attach it to every
   later prompt and add: *"In exactly the same art style, outlines, palette and
   lighting as the attached picture."*
2. **No words, numbers or logos** anywhere. The game writes its own (the number
   over each player's head, too).
3. **Anything marked MAGENTA** goes on flat magenta (#FF00FF), nothing behind it,
   no shadow on the ground. The build cuts the magenta away.
4. **Leave clear space between drawings on a sheet**, and keep the rows the prompt
   asks for. On a crew sheet, every drawing the same height, feet on one line.

## The style (start every prompt with this)

> Detailed 16-bit pixel art for a top-down adventure game seen from slightly above
> (three-quarter view), like a classic Super Nintendo adventure: visible square
> pixels, clean dark outlines, deep ocean blues and teals with glowing cyan and
> warm amber lights, a little eerie but friendly. No text, no letters, no numbers,
> no logos, no border.

## The crew (the suits players pick from)

Deep-sea divers in sleek modern dive suits with a round glass helmet, NOT
cartoon blobs: real proportions, a little chunky, about three heads tall.

**1. Crew A.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of four deep-sea station crew members in sleek dive suits with round glass
> helmets and a small glowing lamp on the shoulder, one per row, six drawings in each
> row: standing facing the viewer, walking facing right (two steps), seen from behind
> working at a panel with both hands, frozen solid inside a block of pale blue ice,
> and a see-through glowing ghost of them floating. Row 1: a coral-red suit. Row 2: a
> sea-green suit. Row 3: a sunshine-yellow suit. Row 4: a violet suit. Every drawing
> the same height with the feet on one line. Flat magenta background.

**2. Crew B.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet layout for four more crew: Row 1: an orange suit. Row 2: a white suit
> with blue trim. Row 3: a black suit with cyan lights. Row 4: a pink suit.

**3. Faces for the picker and the meetings.** MAGENTA.
> A grid of eight square portraits, two rows of four: the same eight crew from the
> attached sheets seen from the shoulders up, their faces visible through the round
> glass helmet, friendly and a little worried. Flat magenta background.

**4. The Mimic.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of a deep-sea creature that disguises itself as crew, eerie but not gory or
> scary for kids: Row 1, three drawings: a crew suit with glowing teal eyes inside the
> helmet; the suit splitting open into soft glowing tentacles; the creature fully
> revealed, a sleek dark-blue cuttlefish-like shape with glowing spots. Row 2, two
> drawings: a curling frosty tentacle reaching sideways; a burst of ice crystals.
> Flat magenta background.

## The station

**5. Floors.** Not magenta. A grid, edge to edge.
> A grid of eight square top-down seamless floor textures for a sea-floor station, two
> rows of four, evenly lit, nothing on them: brushed metal panels with rivets, metal
> grating with dark water below, glass floor over the deep sea with a fish below, rubber
> lab tiles, wet concrete of a sub dock, a carpet with a wave pattern, rusty maintenance
> plates, glowing cyan floor guide lights.

**6. Walls.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of wall pieces for a top-down station seen from slightly above, three rows with
> clear space between them: Row 1, a straight metal wall, a wall with a round porthole
> showing the dark sea, a big curved glass wall with the ocean and kelp behind it. Row 2,
> an inside corner, an outside corner, a sliding door closed, the same door open. Row 3,
> a wall pipe section, a wall with a flickering lamp, a hatch in the floor (a crawl
> tunnel) closed and open. Flat magenta background.

**7. Rooms' things.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of station props seen from slightly above, three rows with clear space between
> them: Row 1, a control desk with screens, a big cylindrical water pump, a glowing reef
> tank, a lab table with sample jars. Row 2, lockers, a small yellow submarine in a dock,
> a sonar beacon on a pedestal with a big round button on top, a cargo crate. Row 3, a
> coiled hose, a fan in a floor grate, a potted sea plant, a bench. Flat magenta background.

**8. The ocean outside.** Not magenta. Wide (21:9), edge to edge.
> A wide deep-ocean scene seen through glass, dark blue fading to black at the bottom,
> light rays from above, kelp forests and rocks in silhouette, a few distant glowing
> jellyfish. It must repeat side to side.

**9. Sea life.** MAGENTA.
> Eight small sea creatures in two rows of four, side view facing right, to swim past
> windows: a silver fish, a school of tiny fish, a glowing jellyfish, a sea turtle, a
> ray, a curious octopus, an anglerfish with a glowing lure, a seahorse. Flat magenta
> background.

## The jobs (close-up screens)

**10. Job panels.** Not magenta. 16:9, edge to edge.
> Four close-up panels side by side for small repair jobs on a sea-floor station, seen
> straight on, each in a metal frame, nothing written on them: a panel of loose colored
> wires with sockets, a tray of glowing crystals in mixed colors with empty slots, a big
> red valve wheel with a pressure gauge (no numbers), a sample scanner with a glass
> slot and a green scan light.

## Meetings and the front

**11. Meeting room.** Not magenta. 16:9, edge to edge.
> A round meeting room on a sea-floor station seen from above at an angle: a big round
> table with a glowing map of the station on it, empty seats around it, a huge round
> window behind showing the deep sea, warm amber lights. No people.

**12. Title picture.** Not magenta. 16:9, edge to edge.
> A sea-floor research station at night seen from outside: glass domes and tubes lit
> warm amber on the dark ocean floor, kelp and rocks, light rays from far above, and
> in one window a crew member silhouette with glowing teal eyes. Leave the top third as
> dark water for the title. No text.
