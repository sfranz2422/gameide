# Snowcap Scramble: picture prompts for Gemini

The twelfth showcase game: an endless climb up an icy mountain, with the class in
one room. Everyone climbs the same mountain, and the board shows who has got
highest. Players throw snowballs that knock each other about a little, and a fall
can go all the way back to the bottom. (The name can change; say if you'd like
another.)

Every picture will have a stand-in drawn by `build.py`, so the game plays without
any of these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like. Anything missing stays a
stand-in.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then tell me which ones are new. Gemini never uses our names, so I look at each one
and name it myself. They get copied into `showcase/climber/art/` and committed, like
Hollowmere's, so they can't be lost.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything.** Make picture 1 first, then attach it to every
   later prompt and add: *"In exactly the same art style, outlines, palette and
   lighting as the attached picture."*
2. **No words, numbers or logos** anywhere. The game writes its own, including the
   number over each player's head.
3. **Anything marked MAGENTA** goes on flat magenta (#FF00FF), with nothing else
   behind it and no shadow on the ground. The build cuts the magenta away.
4. **Leave clear space between drawings on a sheet**, so they can be cut apart, and
   keep them in the rows the prompt asks for.
5. **Same size and same feet:** on a climber sheet, every drawing should be the same
   height, with the feet on the same line. Climbers face RIGHT; the game flips them.

## The style (start every prompt with this)

> Clean, chunky 16-bit pixel art for a side-view platform game, like a classic Super
> Nintendo game: visible square pixels, bold dark outlines, bright cold blues and
> whites with warm accents, cheerful and wintry. No text, no letters, no numbers, no
> logos, no border.

## The climbers (the pictures students pick from)

Each sheet is four climbers, one per row. In every row the same six drawings, all
side view facing RIGHT: **standing, running step 1, running step 2, jumping up,
falling with arms up, throwing a snowball overhand**. Small and cute, about two and a
half heads tall.

**1. Climbers A.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of four small cute pixel art mountain climbers, one per row, six drawings
> in each row, all side view facing right: standing, running step one, running step
> two, jumping up, falling with arms up, throwing a snowball overhand. Row 1: a kid in
> a red puffy jacket and a yellow bobble hat. Row 2: a kid in a teal parka with the
> hood up and fur trim. Row 3: a kid in a purple ski suit and goggles on the forehead.
> Row 4: a kid in an orange coat, green scarf and earmuffs. Every drawing the same
> height with the feet on one line. Flat magenta background.

**2. Climbers B.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet layout for four more climbers: Row 1: a penguin in a tiny blue
> scarf. Row 2: a polar bear cub in a red knitted hat. Row 3: a snow fox with a
> striped scarf. Row 4: a small yeti, white and fluffy with a friendly face.

**3. Faces for the picker and the board.** MAGENTA.
> A grid of eight square head-and-shoulders portraits looking at the viewer, smiling,
> no frames, in two rows of four, the same eight climbers as the attached sheets: the
> red jacket kid, the teal parka kid, the purple ski suit kid, the orange coat kid,
> the penguin, the polar bear cub, the snow fox, the small yeti. Flat magenta
> background.

## Snowballs

**4. Snowball and splat.** MAGENTA.
> A small sheet, side by side with space between: a round snowball, the same snowball
> slightly squashed as if flying fast, a burst of snow as a snowball splats (three
> drawings: small, big, fading puffs), and a little ring of cartoon stars for being
> knocked. Flat magenta background.

## Things to climb on

**5. Ledges.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of side-view platform pieces for a climbing game, in four rows with clear
> space between them, each piece about four times as wide as it is tall. Row 1: an
> icy blue ledge in three pieces, left end, middle (that repeats seamlessly), right
> end. Row 2: a snowy grey rock ledge in the same three pieces. Row 3: one short ice
> ledge three times: whole, cracked, breaking apart into chunks. Row 4: a wooden plank
> hanging from two ropes, and a floating block of ice. Flat magenta background.

**6. The mountain wall.** Not magenta. Edge to edge.
> A tall seamless texture of a frozen rocky cliff face, seen straight on, grey-blue
> stone with snow in the cracks and some ice. It must repeat seamlessly top to bottom
> and side to side. Evenly lit, nothing in front of it, no ledges, no plants.

**7. Things on the mountain.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of side-view decorations for a snowy mountain, in two rows with clear space
> between them. Row 1: a row of hanging icicles, a small snowy pine tree, a tall snowy
> pine tree, a cluster of glowing blue ice crystals. Row 2: a flag on a pole with a
> blank flag, a pile of snowballs, a wooden signpost with no writing, a tiny snow
> bird. Flat magenta background.

## The bottom and the sky

**8. Base camp.** MAGENTA.
> A side-view base camp at the foot of a mountain, as one wide picture: a snowy wooden
> cabin with a lit window, two small tents, a campfire, a sled leaning on the cabin,
> and snowy ground along the bottom edge. Flat magenta background above and around
> it.

**9. Sky, three heights.** Not magenta. Three separate pictures, each wide and edge to edge.
> Three wide side-view sky backgrounds for a climbing game, the same width, with no
> mountains and no ground: 1, a bright morning sky with soft clouds; 2, a golden
> sunset sky with pink clouds; 3, a deep night sky with stars and green northern
> lights. Each must repeat seamlessly left to right.

**10. Far mountains.** MAGENTA.
> A wide side-view strip of distant snowy mountain peaks, pale blue and misty, flat
> along the bottom, that repeats seamlessly left to right. Flat magenta sky above it.

**11. Clouds.** MAGENTA.
> Six separate fluffy pixel art clouds of different sizes, in two rows with clear
> space between them. Flat magenta background.

## Title

**12. Title picture.** Not magenta. Edge to edge, wide (16:9).
> A wide title picture for a cheerful winter climbing game: the eight climbers from
> the attached sheets scrambling up a tall icy mountain, a few snowballs flying
> between them, a blank flag at the very top, morning sky. Leave plain sky in the top
> third for the game to write its title. No text.
