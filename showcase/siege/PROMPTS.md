# Tabletop Siege: picture prompts for Gemini

A battle across a kid's desk: toy soldiers, wind-up knights, paper planes,
rubber-band archers and eraser golems, blue against red, knocking down each
other's pencil-cup towers. Every picture has a stand-in drawn by the game, so it
plays without any of these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then tell me. I copy them into `showcase/siege/raw/` straight away.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything.** Make picture 1 first, then attach it to every
   later prompt and add: *"In exactly the same art style, outlines, palette and
   lighting as the attached picture."*
2. **No words, numbers or logos** anywhere.
3. **Anything marked MAGENTA** goes on flat magenta (#FF00FF), nothing behind it,
   no shadow on the ground, and clear space between drawings. Rows as asked.

## The style (start every prompt with this)

> Bright, chunky 16-bit pixel art for a top-down strategy game seen from slightly
> above (three-quarter view): visible square pixels, clean dark outlines, warm
> sunny colours, toys and school supplies on a wooden desk, cheerful and cute.
> No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border.

## The troops

**1. Blue team.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of blue-painted toy troops, five rows, three drawings in each row, all
> facing right: Row 1, a little toy soldier with a blue helmet: walking (two steps),
> then swinging a tiny toy sword. Row 2, a wind-up tin knight in blue armour with a
> big brass wind-up key on its back: walking (two steps), then swinging a mace.
> Row 3, a folded blue paper plane: flying, tilted, and diving. Row 4, a small archer
> with a rubber-band slingshot and blue cap: walking (two steps), then shooting.
> Row 5, a big chunky pink eraser golem with a blue band: walking (two steps), then
> stomping. Flat magenta background.

**2. Red team.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet with every troop red instead of blue (red helmet, red armour, red
> paper, red cap, a red band on the eraser). Same rows, same poses, same size.

## The towers

**3. Towers and keeps.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet, three rows: Row 1, a round blue pencil cup full of colourful pencils (a
> tower): whole, cracked, and knocked over in a heap. Row 2, the same in red.
> Row 3, a blue lunchbox castle with a handle and a little flag, then the same in
> red, then a heap of broken lunchbox. Flat magenta background.

## The desk

**4. Desk wood.** Not magenta. Square, edge to edge.
> A seamless top-down texture of a light wooden school desk, warm honey colour with a
> soft grain, a few faint pencil marks. Evenly lit, nothing on it. It must repeat.

**5. Desk things.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of school supplies seen from above, three rows with clear space between:
> Row 1, a long wooden ruler lying flat, a second ruler, a pair of scissors.
> Row 2, a sticky note, a paperclip, a few coins, a crayon, a pencil sharpener.
> Row 3, a stack of toy building blocks in four colours (a wall), a glue bottle, a
> white glue puddle, a blue marble and a red marble. Flat magenta background.

**6. Little effects.** MAGENTA.
> Small effects in one row: a cartoon dust puff, a star-shaped hit spark, a stretched
> rubber band flying, a pencil-shaving shot, a brass wind-up key on its own, a
> sparkle burst. Flat magenta background.

## The cards and the front

**7. Card pictures.** MAGENTA.
> Eight square portraits in two rows of four, each a close-up of one of these in the
> same style: a group of three blue toy soldiers; the blue wind-up knight; the blue
> paper plane; the blue rubber-band archer; the eraser golem; a wall of toy blocks;
> a blue marble rolling; a white glue puddle with the bottle. Flat magenta background.

**8. Title picture.** Not magenta. 16:9, edge to edge.
> A kid's wooden desk seen from above at an angle, a toy battle across it: blue toy
> soldiers and a wind-up knight on the left, red ones on the right, paper planes
> overhead, pencil-cup towers at each end, a ruler bridging two pushed-together
> desks, warm afternoon light from a window. Leave the top third simple for a title.
