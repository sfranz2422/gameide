# Snowfort Flags: picture prompts for Gemini

A capture-the-flag snowball fight in a big snowy park, seen from above. Two teams,
Red and Blue, each with a snow fort and a flag. Sneak through the pines, grab their
flag, carry it home. A snowball knocks you down for a moment, never hurts.

Every picture will have a stand-in drawn by the build, so the game plays without
any of these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then tell me which are new. I copy them into `showcase/flags/raw/` straight away,
so they can't be lost.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything.** Make picture 1 first, then attach it to every
   later prompt and add: *"In exactly the same art style, outlines, palette and
   lighting as the attached picture."*
2. **No words, numbers or logos** anywhere. The game writes its own (the number
   over each player's head, too).
3. **Anything marked MAGENTA** goes on flat magenta (#FF00FF), nothing behind it,
   no shadow on the ground. The build cuts the magenta away.
4. **Leave clear space between drawings on a sheet**, and keep the rows the prompt
   asks for. On a kid sheet, every drawing the same height, feet on one line.

## The style (start every prompt with this)

> Detailed 16-bit pixel art for a top-down adventure game seen from slightly above
> (three-quarter view), like a classic Super Nintendo adventure: visible square
> pixels, clean dark outlines, crisp winter whites and pale blues with warm reds and
> bright blues for the two teams, cheerful and cosy. No text, no letters, no numbers,
> no logos, no border.

## The kids (each team has four looks, so players can tell each other apart)

Kids about ten years old in puffy winter coats, real proportions, a little chunky,
about three heads tall. Not chibi, not blobs.

**1. Red team, walking.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of four kids in RED winter coats, one kid per row, six drawings in each
> row: standing facing the viewer, walking facing the viewer, standing seen from
> behind, walking seen from behind, standing facing right, walking facing right.
> Row 1: a red puffer coat and a white bobble hat. Row 2: a red parka with a fur hood
> up. Row 3: a red coat, a striped scarf and earmuffs. Row 4: a red snowsuit and a
> knitted hat with ear flaps. Every drawing the same height with the feet on one line.
> Flat magenta background.

**2. Red team, in action.** MAGENTA.
> The same four red-team kids, one per row, six drawings in each row: throwing a
> snowball to the right (arm back), throwing a snowball to the right (arm forward),
> throwing a snowball away from the viewer, crouching to scoop snow into a ball,
> knocked down sitting in the snow, dizzy with a little puff of snow, and cheering
> with both arms up. Every drawing the same height with the feet on one line.
> Flat magenta background.

**3. Blue team, walking.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet layout as the attached red-team sheet, for four kids in BLUE winter
> coats: Row 1: a blue puffer coat and a yellow bobble hat. Row 2: a blue parka with a
> fur hood up. Row 3: a blue coat, a striped scarf and a beanie. Row 4: a blue
> snowsuit and goggles pushed up on a hat.

**4. Blue team, in action.** MAGENTA.
> The same action sheet layout as the attached red-team action sheet, for the same
> four blue-team kids.

## The forts and the flags

**5. Forts and flags.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of game objects seen from slightly above: Row 1: a big snow fort with
> crenellated snow-brick walls and a gap for a gate, decorated with RED ribbons; the
> same fort decorated with BLUE ribbons. Row 2: a red flag on a wooden pole stuck in
> the snow, a blue flag on a wooden pole, a red flag lying dropped in the snow, a blue
> flag lying dropped in the snow, an empty flag stand of snow. Flat magenta background.

## The park

**6. Ground tiles.** (no magenta; a flat sheet)
> A tileset sheet of square ground tiles in a 4 by 4 grid, each tile seamless with
> its neighbours, seen from straight above: fresh deep snow (two variations), trodden
> snow with footprints, a packed snow path, frozen creek ice (two variations), the
> creek's snowy bank on the top, bottom, left and right sides, a wooden plank bridge
> across ice, a snowy grass patch, icy puddle, and snow with little twigs and stones.

**7. Trees and banks.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of winter scenery seen from slightly above: Row 1: three snowy pine trees of
> different sizes, a bare snowy oak. Row 2: a long snowbank, a round snow drift with a
> scoop taken out of it, a snowy hedge, a frozen bush with red berries. Row 3: a
> snowman with a carrot nose, a wooden sled, a park bench covered in snow, a lamp post
> with a warm glow. Flat magenta background.

**8. Snowballs.** MAGENTA.
> A small sheet: a snowball (three sizes), a snowball flying with a little trail, a
> snowball bursting into a splat of powder (three frames, getting bigger), a footprint
> pair in snow, a pile of three snowballs. Flat magenta background.

## The title

**9. Title picture.** (no magenta; fills the whole picture, wide, 16:9)
> A wide winter park at sunset seen from slightly above: a red snow fort on the left
> and a blue snow fort on the right, each with its flag, kids from both teams ducking
> behind snowbanks and throwing snowballs in the middle, pine forest and a frozen
> creek between them, soft pink and gold sky. Leave the top third fairly plain for the
> game's title.
