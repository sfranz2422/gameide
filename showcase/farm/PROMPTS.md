# Cloverbrook: picture prompts for Gemini

A farm that grows into a town: grow crops, raise animals, make goods in factories,
fill orders, build houses and watch townspeople move in, and clear new land as the
valley opens up. Everything is seen from above at a slight angle (top-down 3/4, like
a classic farming game), on a square grid.

The game is built in phases, and so are its pictures: **each phase's are needed
when that phase is built.** Every picture has a stand-in drawn by the build, so the game plays without any
of these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then tell me which are new. I copy them into `showcase/farm/raw/` straight away, so
they can't be lost.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything, and nothing else in it.** Make picture 1 first,
   then attach it to every later prompt and add: *"In exactly the same art style,
   outlines, palette, lighting and viewing angle as the attached picture."*
   (Mix Lab's sheets came back inside the wooden frame of an earlier picture in the
   chat: a chat of its own keeps each picture to what its prompt asks.)
2. **No words, numbers or logos** anywhere, not on signs, crates or the truck.
3. **Anything marked MAGENTA** goes on flat magenta (#FF00FF), the same magenta all
   over, nothing behind each drawing, no shadow on the ground, no frame or border
   around the sheet. The build cuts the magenta away.
4. **Leave clear space between drawings on a sheet**, and keep the rows the prompt
   asks for, in its order.
5. **The same angle for everything:** seen from above, tilted so the fronts of
   buildings and the near side of fields show, the way a classic farming game looks.

## The style (start every prompt with this)

> Bright, cheerful cartoon art for a cosy farming and town-building game, seen from
> above at a slight angle (top-down three-quarter view, like a classic farming game):
> clean dark outlines, soft shading, warm sunny light from the top left, rich friendly
> colours. Not pixel art, not a photo. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

## Phase 1: the farm

**1. Crops.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] A sheet of square farm fields, each one the same size: a square patch of
> dark brown plowed soil with neat rows, seen from above at the same slight angle,
> with plants growing in it. Four rows, four fields in each row, showing the crop
> growing from left to right: tiny sprouts, young plants, nearly grown, ready to
> harvest. Row 1: wheat (ripe golden wheat at the end). Row 2: corn (tall stalks with
> yellow cobs at the end). Row 3: carrots (leafy green tops with orange carrots
> peeking out at the end). Row 4: pumpkins (vines with big orange pumpkins at the end).
> Clear space between fields. Flat magenta background.

**2. Fields and nature.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] A sheet of farm ground pieces and nature, two rows of six, each on its
> own. Row 1: an empty square of dark plowed soil (the same size and shape as the
> fields in the attached picture), the same square of soil darker and wet as if just
> watered, a square patch of grass with a few small flowers, a short square piece of
> dirt path, a wooden fence section, a wooden gate. Row 2: a big leafy oak tree, a
> tall pine tree, a large grey boulder, a small round bush with berries, an old tree
> stump, a little pond with lily pads. Flat magenta background.

**3. Farm buildings.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] A sheet of farm buildings, two rows of three, each on its own, each
> standing on a little square of grass so you can see its footprint. Row 1: a big red
> barn with white trim and big doors, a cosy farmhouse with a porch and a chimney, a
> tall metal grain silo. Row 2: a wooden order board on two posts with paper orders
> pinned to it, a small blue delivery truck parked (seen from the front-left), a
> roadside farm stand with a striped awning. Flat magenta background.

**4. Goods and icons.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] A sheet of small round-cornered game icons, each one a single object on
> its own, all the same size, four rows of five. Row 1: a bundle of wheat, a corn cob,
> a bunch of carrots, a pumpkin, a small sack of seeds. Row 2: a gold coin, a gold
> star, a green arrow pointing up, a padlock, an alarm clock. Row 3: a sickle, a
> watering can, a wooden crate, a red barn door (storage), a rolled paper order with
> a ribbon. Row 4: a hammer, a saw, an axe, a pickaxe, a shovel. Flat magenta
> background.

**5. The title picture.** (no magenta)
> [the style] A wide (16:9) view of a cosy farming valley in morning sunshine: golden
> fields in rows, a red barn, a farmhouse, a winding dirt road leading to a small
> village with colourful houses in the distance, green hills, a few fluffy clouds, a
> clear blue sky in the top third with nothing in it (the game writes its name there).

## Phase 2: animals and factories

built to match, so these must be isometric too, at the same angle. Use the same Gemini
chat as Phase 1, attach picture 2 (the farm buildings) to every prompt, and add:
*"Isometric, at exactly the same angle, scale, style, outlines and lighting as the attached
picture. Each one standing on its own diamond of grass, like the attached buildings."*

> shed with an open side and a fenced grassy yard in front; a sheep pen with a small

## Phase 3: the town

Isometric again, in the same Gemini chat. Attach **picture 3** (the farm buildings) to
every prompt, and add: *"Isometric, at exactly the same angle, scale, style, outlines and
lighting as the attached picture. Each one standing on its own diamond of grass, like the
attached buildings."* Ask for **only what the prompt lists**: picture 6 came back with the
order board, truck and stands drawn again underneath.

**11. Houses.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Isometric. A sheet of four houses for a little farming town, in ONE row,
> each standing on its own grass diamond the same size as the barn's in the attached
> picture, with clear space between them, from smallest to biggest: a tiny cosy cottage
> with a thatched roof and a flower box; a family house with a blue roof and a little
> front garden; a narrow two-storey brick townhouse with a chimney; a big white manor
> house with a porch and tall windows. Nothing else on the sheet. Flat magenta background.

**12. Town buildings.** MAGENTA.
> [the style] Isometric. A sheet of six town buildings, two rows of three, each standing
> on its own grass diamond the same size as the barn's in the attached picture, with clear
> space between them. Row 1: a stone fountain in a little round paved square with flower
> beds; a small park with two trees, a bench and a winding path; a red brick school with a
> bell on the roof. Row 2: a café with a striped awning and little tables outside; a town
> hall with a clock tower (the clock has no numbers); a library with steps and columns.
> Nothing else on the sheet. Flat magenta background.

**13. Townspeople.** MAGENTA. (Attach picture 7, the animals, as well, for their size.)
> [the style] Small cute townspeople, seen from the same angle, full body, four rows of
> four, each on its own: Row 1: a girl with pigtails. Row 2: a boy in a cap. Row 3: a
> woman in a dress. Row 4: an old man with a walking stick. In every row, the same person
> four times: walking towards you and to the left (two drawings, one with the left foot
> forward, one with the right), then walking away from you and to the left, seen from
> behind (two drawings, the same two steps). No shadows, no ground under them. Flat
> magenta background.

**14. Town icons.** MAGENTA. (Attach picture 4, the icons, as well.)
> [the style] A sheet of small round-cornered game icons in exactly the style of the
> attached icons (the same cream tiles), each one a single object on its own, all the same
> size, ONE row of five: three smiling townspeople's heads together; a little house; a
> short piece of cobblestone road; four arrows pointing out (to move something); a cloth
> bag of gold coins. Flat magenta background.
