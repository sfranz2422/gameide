# Squeeze: picture prompts for Gemini

A lemonade stand game. Each morning you buy lemons, sugar, ice and cups, set your
recipe and price, pick a spot; then the day plays out on a sunny street seen from
the side, customers walking past, stopping (or not), drinking, and saying what they
think. 30 days of summer.

Every picture will have a stand-in drawn by the build, so the game plays without
any of these. Do them in order and stop whenever you like.

**Saving them:** leave them in `~/Downloads` under whatever name Gemini gives them,
then tell me which are new. I copy them into `showcase/lemon/raw/` straight away,
so they can't be lost.

## How to keep them all in one style

1. **One Gemini chat for everything.** Make picture 1 first, then attach it to every
   later prompt and add: *"In exactly the same art style, outlines, palette and
   lighting as the attached picture."*
2. **No words, numbers, prices or logos** anywhere, not even on the stand's sign.
   The game writes its own.
3. **Anything marked MAGENTA** goes on flat magenta (#FF00FF), nothing behind it,
   no shadow on the ground. The build cuts the magenta away.
4. **Leave clear space between drawings on a sheet**, and keep the rows the prompt
   asks for. On a people sheet, every drawing the same height, feet on one line.

## The style (start every prompt with this)

> Detailed 16-bit pixel art for a cosy side-view game, like a classic Super Nintendo
> game: visible square pixels, clean dark outlines, bright summer colours (lemon
> yellow, sky blue, leafy green, warm pink), cheerful and friendly. Seen from the
> side, straight on. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no border.

## The stand

**1. The lemonade stand.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of a wooden lemonade stand seen from the front, side view, four versions
> in one row, each the same size: a simple wooden stand with a yellow-and-white
> striped awning and a pitcher on the counter; the same stand with a big blank
> hanging sign on top; the same stand with a blue cooler beside it and a juicer on
> the counter; the same stand painted pink with a pink awning and pink lemonade in
> the pitcher. Flat magenta background.

**2. You, at the stand.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of one kid about ten years old in a yellow apron and a sun hat, standing
> behind a counter so only the top half shows (the counter itself not drawn), one
> row of six drawings: smiling and waiting, pouring from a pitcher, handing over a
> cup, cheering with both arms up, wiping their forehead in the heat, looking worried.
> Flat magenta background.

## The customers (side view, walking from left to right)

Real proportions, not chibi, about four heads tall for adults.

**3. Customers A.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of four different people, one per row, five drawings in each row: walking
> to the right (two steps), standing facing right, drinking from a paper cup, and
> happy with a big smile. Row 1: a jogger in a headband and running shorts. Row 2: a
> grandmother with a sun hat and a handbag. Row 3: an office worker in a shirt and
> tie with a briefcase. Row 4: a kid with a skateboard under one arm. Every drawing
> the same height scale with the feet on one line. Flat magenta background.

**4. Customers B.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet layout for four more people: Row 1: a tourist with a camera and a
> floral shirt. Row 2: a dog walker (the small dog walks beside them). Row 3: a
> builder in a hard hat and a high-visibility vest. Row 4: a teenager with
> headphones.

**5. Customers C.** MAGENTA.
> The same sheet layout for four more people: Row 1: a mum pushing a stroller. Row 2:
> a beach-goer in a swimsuit with a towel over the shoulder. Row 3: a baseball fan in
> a cap and jersey with a foam finger. Row 4: a mail carrier with a bag.

## The places (wide backgrounds, no magenta)

Each one wide (16:9), seen from the side, with a flat pavement or path along the
bottom fifth where people walk, and an empty spot in the middle for the stand.

**6. The park.**
> A sunny city park seen from the side: green grass, big leafy trees, a pond in the
> distance, a park bench, flowers, a flat path along the bottom. Leave the middle of
> the path clear.

**7. The beach.**
> A sunny beach boardwalk seen from the side: blue sea and waves, sand, beach
> umbrellas and a lifeguard tower in the distance, a flat wooden boardwalk along the
> bottom. Leave the middle of the boardwalk clear.

**8. The ballpark.**
> Outside a baseball stadium seen from the side: the stadium's brick wall and lights
> behind, banners with no words, a flat pavement along the bottom. Leave the middle
> of the pavement clear.

**9. Downtown.**
> A busy downtown street seen from the side: shops with awnings and no words on
> them, a bus stop, street lamps, tall buildings behind, a flat pavement along the
> bottom. Leave the middle of the pavement clear.

## Things

**10. Supplies and upgrades.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of game icons, all the same size, two rows: Row 1: a lemon, a bag of
> sugar, a bag of ice, a stack of paper cups, a pitcher of lemonade, a pitcher of pink
> lemonade, a gold coin. Row 2: a blue cooler, a hand juicer, a hanging sign with no
> words, a second small stand, a strawberry, a soft brown lemon gone bad. Flat magenta
> background.

**11. Thought bubbles.** MAGENTA.
> A sheet of six small round thought bubbles with a little tail, each with one simple
> picture inside: a puckered sour face, a face sticking its tongue out at too much
> sugar, a melting ice cube with a sad face, a coin with wings flying away (too
> pricey), a heart, a sweating face (too hot and thirsty). Flat magenta background.

## The title

**12. Title picture.** (no magenta; wide, 16:9)
> A cheerful summer street at golden hour seen from the side: a lemonade stand with a
> striped awning in the middle, a kid behind it pouring lemonade, a short queue of
> happy customers, lemons in a basket, a bright sun. Leave the top third fairly
> plain for the game's title.
